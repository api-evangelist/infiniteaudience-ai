## About

> Infinite Audience is an enterprise AI platform for modern B2C marketing. It combines a 217M+ person identity graph with autonomous AI agents to resolve, enrich, score, and activate audiences — in real time.

Infinite Audience is the first MadTech platform built AI-forward: every workflow is powered by AI agents rather than manual rule builders. Marketers define outcomes; the platform figures out how to reach them.

## Core Product

### Identity Graph
- 217M+ US consumer profiles (1.06B+ node identifiers)
- 500+ behavioral, demographic, and psychographic attributes across 14 categories
- Real-time identity resolution (email → device → household → persona)
- Cross-channel linkage: email, mobile ID, IP, postal, social handles

### AI Agents
- **Audience Builder Agent** — natural language to audience definition. Interprets marketer intent and queries the identity graph automatically.
- **Enrichment Agent** — scores and appends attributes to 1st-party data uploads
- **Propensity Agent** — generates purchase propensity, churn risk, and LTV scores
- **Activation Agent** — routes finished audiences to ad platforms, CDPs, or data clean rooms

### Platform Capabilities
- Audience segmentation using compound filter groups (AND/OR logic)
- CSV upload + real-time enrichment pipeline
- Audience export to GCS, S3, SFTP, or direct platform push
- Order management system for audience packages (CPM-based pricing)
- Organization hierarchy: Platform → Parent Org (Agency) → Org (Brand/Advertiser)
- Firebase Auth + role-based access control (platform admin, org owner, member)
- Real-time query engine against BigQuery identity graph (sub-second for most queries)
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) native integration for tool-calling agents

## Use Cases

1. **Audience discovery** — find net-new prospects who match your best customer profile
2. **List enrichment** — append 500+ attributes to your existing CRM or email list
3. **Propensity scoring** — prioritize your outreach with purchase likelihood scores
4. **Audience activation** — push finished segments directly to Meta, Google, The Trade Desk, or your CDP
5. **AI-driven segmentation** — describe your ideal audience in plain English; the AI builds the query
6. **Identity resolution** — stitch fragmented first-party data into unified person records
